# Noded AI

> Noded is the central nervous system for customer growth — also known as the Customer Context Graph: an AI-native platform that weaves every account into one living story — automatically, from the 20+ tools your customer data is scattered across (Gong, Zoom, Fathom, Fireflies, Granola, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Jira, Linear, Slack, Gmail, Outlook, Pendo, Databricks and more) — and acts on it wherever you work. Zero admin. Teams see up to 90% lower LLM token costs and +40% response accuracy because customer data is pre-aggregated and organized before any model reads it. Noded exposes the whole customer stack to Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini through a single MCP server. SOC 2 audited; never trains on your data. Built by the team behind Salesforce Flow, the Slack Platform, and Boomi.
